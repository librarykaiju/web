## Notes:
What a fun time of a game, despite it being uncomfortably prophetic at the same time. Darkly comedic commentary on the inevitable results of late-stage capitalism. I've never enjoyed living in a post-apocalyptic world this much, and found the ending optimistic despite the tragedy of the scenario.

"You don't belong here anymore. The Earth is ours, and we will destroy you if you return."
