## Summary & Notes

_After a Black Plague orphan predicts a coming apocalypse, Thomas, a disgraced knight, finds himself in the middle of a second war on heaven as angels fight demons._

- Some scenes read like an A24 film where the setting and environment make you ask yourself, "is this real or am I watching a slow descent into madness?"

- In spite of this, the author cements the gothic fantasy elements by conveying the cosmic reality at play via short vignettes between Heaven and Hell that read like _Dante's Inferno_.

- Thomas' journey of redemption and forgiveness was beautiful.
