## Summary & Notes

Tam Hashford has always dreamed of living through glory days of her own. With a renowned mercenary for a father and an illustrious bard for a mother, battles and adventure seem the only way to really live. So when she learns that the most revered mercenary crew in Grandual is in need of a bard, she grabs her lute and goes on tour. Led by the infamous Bloody Rose, Tam and her new band embark on a mission that will earn them everlasting fame -- or certain death.
