## Summary & Notes

Such a fun read. "Disco Space Opera" could be a whole genre of sci-fi.

- The characters were unique and well developed.

- The world is rich in that way that is revealed more through conversation than exposition. Humanity are "strangers in a strange land" and the lines between mysticism and science are blurred in many places. Or maybe this is going the Warhammer route where the two co-exist and co-mingle.

- Thing is by far my favorite character.
