## Summary & Notes

Carl and Donut have survived so far, but this fourth level is unlike anything they could imagine. The Iron Tangle: an impossibly complicated subway system tied together into a knot of trains of all kinds, from classic steam engines to sleek modern cars. Up is down. Down is up. Close is far. The cars are filled with monsters, the railway stations aren’t always what they seem, and the exit is perpetually just a few stops away.  
  
The top ten list is populated, and Carl and Donut have made it. But that popularity comes with a price. They each now have a bounty on their head. They must work with other crawlers to solve the puzzle of the floor, but how can they do that when they don’t know who to trust? The secret to unraveling it all may be hidden in the pages of a seemingly useless book.

- I liked the expanded cast of characters in this one.

- Loved the reveal the Carl has inherited this body of knowledge that Crawlers have been compiling for years in order to beat the system they are all trapped in. Love that Carl is self-aware enough to understand that he can't do it alone, and starts making connections with other crawlers and coordinating information sharing in the dungeon.

- Loved the resolution of conflict with Hekla. It's so unexpected and yet so satisfying while setting up further complications down the road.

- Loved seeing Donut go absolutely apeshit for her people.
