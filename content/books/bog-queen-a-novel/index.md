## Summary & Notes

_A monumental discovery sets off a clash of worlds, past and present, over the fate of the land that holds us._

- While not a fantasy, the book features beautifully written monologues in the voice of the Bog that give this story a slight flavor of magical realism. Both indifferent and alien to the human experience, they serve as a reminder that no matter what happens, the Earth will endure long after we are gone.

- This book takes its time, and lives in the skin of its characters. Not a quick read, but engaging, nonetheless.

- Fucking.patriarchy.
