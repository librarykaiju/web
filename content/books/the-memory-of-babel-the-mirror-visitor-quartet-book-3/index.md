## Summary & Notes

Ophelia, hoping to put what she has learned from the Book of Farouk to good use, uses an assumed identity to travel through mirrors to the ark of Babel, but she needs to use all of her skills as a reader to avoid being lured into a trap set by mysterious adversaries.
