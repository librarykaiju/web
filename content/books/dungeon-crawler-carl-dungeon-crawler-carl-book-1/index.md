## Summary & Notes

You know what’s worse than breaking up with your girlfriend? Being stuck with her prize-winning show cat. And you know what’s worse than that? An alien invasion, the destruction of all man-made structures on Earth, and the systematic exploitation of all the survivors for a sadistic intergalactic game show. That’s what.

- Fun tone in the writing. The story is as absurd as the premise, which is okay. The author still finds quiet moments for Carl's introspection and growth.

- Narrator Jeff Hays has ridiculous range, playing Carl as a stoic homage to Patrick Warburton and Princess Donut exactly how you would imagine a sassy show-cat sounds in your head.

- Like a lot of LitRPG stuff, the exposition can get a bit technical, which bogs things down a little. The author works around this by characterizing the game system as a snark AI.
