## Summary & Notes

The aliens have come, and they’ve transformed Earth into a multilevel, video game–like dungeon. It’s the newest season of the galaxy’s most watched game show, Dungeon Crawler World. Now on the third floor, Carl and Donut have to fight harder than ever. Viewers can’t get enough. But the dungeon gets more dangerous each day, and now there’s a whole new problem to deal with: They call it the Over City. A sprawling, once-thriving metropolis devastated by a mysterious calamity. But these streets are far from abandoned. Can Carl and Donut solve the mystery in time? And can Carl finally find some pants?

- The 2nd books introduces the concept of NPC story lines that are happening alongside the game show of Dungeon Crawler World. This adds a layer of complexity to Carl's and Donut's whole predicament, which adds some fun drama that is setting up something bigger down the road.

- Carl and Donut continue to be a fun duo, and Jeff Hays continues to deliver performances that are both funny and endearing.

- In many ways Carl is very much designed as an action hero archetype, but he approaches problems in this book with emotional intelligence that breaks with what you would expect in either a video game or movie. Loved how he handled Grimaldi's Circus.
