The other night I was helping my son with his writing assignment and he randomly asked, "Dad, are there any words with more than five syllables?"

Being something of a logophile, I immediately started jotting down muli-syllabic words to see how long it took me to find one. 

It took 4:

- ex-ped-i-tion - 5 syllables.
- meta-mor-pho-sis - 5 syllables.
- hom-eo-sta-sis - 5 syllables.
- Tris-kai-dec-a-pho-bi-a - 7 syllables.

After doing the work, Linc pipes up, "Hippopotomonstrosesquipedaliophobia is one I know." 

Hip-po-pot-o-mon-stro-ses-qui-pe-da-li-o-pho-bi-a is a 15 syllable word.
