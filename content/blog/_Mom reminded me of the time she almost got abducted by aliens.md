I went to go see Mom and Duane today before work. Duane seems to be doing better after a rough time last week. Mom seemed tired, but she was in good spirits. I took over some Adobo Lauren made for them and she was excited about it.

While hanging out with Mom a Kit Kat commercial came on TV. I missed the context of the ad, but at the end of it a flying saucer came onto the screen and beamed away one of sticks of the candy bar.

"UFO's," mom said. "Why UFOs, I wonder?"

"Because UFOs are cool?" I ventured.

We sat in silence for a beat and then she asked, "Do you remember the story about my UFO experience?"

I blinked. Holy shit. I completely forgot about it.

Holy shit," I said. "I completely forgot about it." 

Though how, I'll never know. This story is the kind of family lore that leaves an impression with the first telling, and she must have told it to me dozens of times over the years. When I was young, I remember listening in rapt attention, with the awe, fascination, one feels when an authority figure tells you both fascinated and terrified. The details of the story all came rushing back, but she recounted it again for me anyway.
