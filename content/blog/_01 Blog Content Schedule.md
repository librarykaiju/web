In an effort to get back to writing/posting in a more regular fashion I've come up with a content structure that I feel is fairly workable. Sometimes the output might come from what I’m doing here, sometimes it won’t be.
#### Monday: Pages 
Typical journal entries about my thoughts, struggles, and etc.
#### Tuesday: Favorite Things (quotes, internet videos, and what-have-you)  
#### Wednesday: Made
Sketches, craft projects, and other artistic endeavors

#### Thursday: What’s in the news
Commentary about any entertainment news/current events

#### Friday: Flash Fiction, Five Songs Friday
Mostly music, but maybe the occasional piece of fiction.
