Sir Roland Freehand is the foster father of the Pumpkin Knight. Known to some as Roland Freehand or The Ageless Knight Roland was blessed with long life and wanders the Free Territories helping those in need. He is kind, and quick to laugh, but haunted by several lifetimes of war and a secret that he will never atone for.
