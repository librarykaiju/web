Beren Spark lives at the edge of the Deep Wood with his wife Lua and daughter Celia. He is the proprietor of the Last Home, an Inn at the edge of the forest that waits for adventurers to come and test their meddle. His true purpose in living near the wood is known only to Lua, Roland, and the Fairy Queen Bryghid.
