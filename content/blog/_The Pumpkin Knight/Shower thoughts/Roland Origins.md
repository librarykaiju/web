Born in a humble village, Roland's first taste of battle came at the age of 12 when he was conscripted to defend a fortification known as The Cleft during the first battle that would lead to the Darkling Wars. Bands of goblins, ogres swept through the countryside, forcing villagers to flee for the sanctuary of The Cleft. Roland's family was killed in the migration, but his father put him on their horse and bade him to ride for The Cleft to warn the King of the coming horde.
 
Unable to defend the cleft with their few numbers, anyone who was able-bodied enough to wield a sword was fitted for battle. Roland survived the battle by being too small to notice in the chaos. During the fighting he found himself among the king's guard. Their valor and bravery gave Roland courage and he stood with them
